💡 About ETFs

Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) are investment funds that trade on stock exchanges like a single stock, while holding a basket of assets such as stocks, bonds or commodities.

Categories

  • Broad Market: S&P 500, total US and total world equity.
  • Technology: Nasdaq-100 and technology-sector funds.
  • Sector: single US sectors: financials, energy, health care, industrials, real estate.
  • Bonds: US aggregate and long-duration Treasury bonds.
  • International: developed markets ex-US and emerging markets.
  • Commodities: physical gold and silver.
  • Dividend: high-dividend-yield US equities.

Prices are daily closes (dividend-adjusted) from Yahoo Finance, cached for 5 minutes. Week/Month/Year compare against the last close on or before 7/30/365 calendar days ago.

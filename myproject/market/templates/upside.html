Read before using this list

  • Price targets are analysts' opinions about the next 12 months, and on average they are optimistic.
  • A very large gap usually means the price has fallen and the targets have not caught up. Check the range and the "Updated" column: none means no firm changed or reiterated a target in {{ choices.fresh_days }} days.
  • Few analysts means one opinion can set the whole number. The default hides stocks with fewer than 5.
  • This list has not been tested as a trading signal. Only today's targets are available, so there is no honest backtest yet. Not investment advice.

Targets and prices: Yahoo Finance consensus (mean, median, low, high and analyst count). Stocks: the largest US-listed common shares by market cap, ETFs, OTC and preferred shares excluded. Targets are refreshed daily and prices every 15 minutes.
